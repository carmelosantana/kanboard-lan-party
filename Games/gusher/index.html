Gusher

The well is about to blow. Lay pipe from the queue before the countdown ends, then keep ahead of the crude.

Arrow keys or WASD move, Space or Enter lays the next piece, F opens the throttle. Click or tap a square to lay there. Laying over your own pipe swaps it but lets the crude run on for two seconds. An open end spills and ends the round; ending every line in a depot, or looping it back into itself, seals the well for a bonus.
