Wicklings

Little candle-folk tumble out of the hatch and march on until something turns them. Bring enough of them to the lantern before their wicks burn down.

Pick a job with 1–5 or the job buttons, then click a wickling, or choose one with the arrow keys and press Space. P pauses (jobs can be handed out while paused), S steps one tick while paused, F speeds up.
